A memecoin with a Bitcoin heartbeat

Most memecoins ask you to believe. Buy The Cat just pays. It's a Solana Token-2022 coin — ticker $BTC, a deliberate wink at the original — that turns trading volume into actual Bitcoin for the people holding the bag.

The mechanism is on-chain and boring on purpose: a fixed 3% fee on every transfer is harvested into a shared pot, swapped into wrapped Bitcoin (wBTC), and airdropped to holders in proportion to what they hold. Hold the cat. The cat brings back Bitcoin.

  • 3% fee → a shared pot. Every $BTC transfer — not just trades, but any wallet-to-wallet send — carries a 3% Token-2022 fee that flows into a shared reward pot.
  • The pot converts to wBTC. Collected fees are swapped into wrapped Bitcoin (wBTC) and held in the pot until it's ready to pay out.
  • Auto pro-rata payouts. When the pot reaches 0.1% of market cap, wBTC is distributed automatically by your share of supply. No staking, no claiming, no lockups.
  • Payouts scale with activity. More volume means a fuller pot and larger drips — rewards grow as the ecosystem grows.
  • Authorities renounced, LP locked. Mint & freeze authority renounced, metadata immutable, the 3% fee rate locked forever, liquidity 99% locked.

wBTC is wrapped Bitcoin — a tokenised version of BTC that lives on chains like Solana. It is not native Bitcoin.
